Our Story —
A Legacy of Coastal Luxury

Built on a belief that hospitality is personal.

The Bellavera Resort & Spa began not as a grand vision, but as a quiet conviction: that a guest who feels genuinely cared for will carry that feeling long after checkout. More than fifty years later, that conviction still guides every decision we make.

1972 · The Beginning

Founded in the shade
of a Florida sunset.

Marco and Elena Bellavera arrived in Coral Bay from Verona in the spring of 1971, carrying little more than a shared passion for hospitality and a deep love of the sea. Within a year, they had transformed a modest 40-room inn at the edge of the Atlantic into what Voyager Travel Guide would later call "the most thoughtfully run property on Florida's eastern shore."

Marco believed that luxury was not about marble floors or gold fixtures, but about the feeling a guest carries with them when they leave. Elena ran the kitchens herself for the first three years, establishing the culinary philosophy that still underpins every dish served at The Bellavera today.

Growth & Transformation

Three generations,
one standard.

Through the 1980s, The Bellavera expanded — carefully and deliberately — from 40 rooms to its current 248 rooms and suites. The Crest Collection was added in 2004, and Spa Bellavera opened in 2009 to immediate critical acclaim.

Today, the resort is managed by the third generation of the Bellavera family, with founding daughter Claudia Bellavera serving as Managing Director. The same values Marco penned on a napkin in 1972 — Excellence, Warmth, Presence — are printed in the employee handbook given to every new team member.

The three words
we live by.

Every policy, every hire, every renovation decision at The Bellavera passes through the same three-word filter that Marco wrote down on his first day in Coral Bay.

◈

Excellence

Not perfection — perfection is static. Excellence is active, curious, and always asking how this moment could be made more meaningful for the person in front of us.

◇

Warmth

A hotel can be beautiful without being welcoming. We want guests to feel our warmth before they see our design — in the way the door opens, the way their name is used, the way they're remembered.

✦

Presence

The greatest gift we give our guests is the gift of being fully present. We design every experience to quiet the outside world so that what remains is simply this: the sea, the sky, and the moment.
