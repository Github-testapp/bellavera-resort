The Bellavera

Experiences & Amenities

More than a resort.
A world of its own.

At The Bellavera, the experience begins the moment you step through the door and continues in ways you won't expect. From the stillness of Spa Bellavera to the energy of the championship golf course, every amenity is crafted with the same care as the rooms you sleep in.

Spa & Wellness

Spa Bellavera —
Surrender to stillness.

Spread across two serene floors, Spa Bellavera houses 18 treatment rooms, a thermal hydrotherapy circuit, a private couples suite, and a meditation garden. Our therapists draw on both traditional and contemporary techniques — from deep-tissue massage to our signature Coastal Renewal ritual using locally sourced sea minerals.

Signature Treatments

The Bellavera Coastal Ritual, Sea Mineral Wrap, and Atlantic Restorative Massage are among our most requested — each designed to draw on the healing power of the ocean.

Thermal Circuit

Begin your journey with an hour in our thermal hydrotherapy circuit — salt floatation pool, steam caves, cold plunge, and relaxation loungers overlooking the garden.

Wellness Programmes

Multi-day retreats combining daily spa treatments, guided meditation, nutritional consultations, and private fitness sessions with our resident wellness coach.

Pools & Private Beach

Your own patch
of the Atlantic.

Three resort pools — including an adults-only infinity pool and a family aqua park — give way to half a mile of private Atlantic beachfront attended by our team from sunrise to sunset. Beach chairs, umbrellas, towels, and cold drinks arrive before you ask for them.

Golf Links

18 holes, ocean views.

Our championship course winds through coastal woodland and across elevated fairways with sweeping Atlantic views. PGA-certified instruction, club hire, and a fully appointed clubhouse included.
