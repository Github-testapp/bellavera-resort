Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join The Bellavera Circle?

Pearl membership is awarded automatically after your first stay and requires no sign-up. To enrol in Coral or Crest, please contact our membership team at circle@thebellavera.com or call +1 888-235-4105. We will guide you through the process and ensure your benefits are active before your next visit.

Can I upgrade my membership tier?

Yes. You may upgrade from Pearl to Coral or Crest at any time. The annual fee is prorated from the date of upgrade. Benefits are activated immediately upon confirmation of payment.

Are membership benefits transferable to a guest?

Coral and Crest memberships may be used by the named member only. However, Crest members may nominate one additional guest (a partner or family member) to share full benefits at a reduced add-on rate of $400 per year.

When does my membership renew?

Memberships renew annually on the date of enrollment. You will receive a renewal notice 30 days in advance. If you choose not to renew, your benefits remain active until the last day of your membership year.
