The Bellavera

Dining that raises the bar.

Five restaurants.
Countless reasons to stay at the table.

Our chefs work directly with local fishermen, coastal farms, and artisan producers every morning — because every great meal starts before the kitchen opens. From the open-air Oyster Deck to the intimate Coral Room, dining at The Bellavera is always an occasion.

Rooftop · Mediterranean

Azure Grill

The rooftop where
every night glows.

Wednesday – Sunday  ·  6:00 PM – 11:00 PM

Elevated above the resort on the open-air rooftop terrace, Azure Grill brings together the bold flavours of the Mediterranean and the warmth of the Florida coast. Wood-fired meats, fresh mezze, and an extensive wine list set against panoramic ocean views.

The Coral Room

Intimate. Considered.
Unforgettable.

Tuesday – Saturday  ·  6:00 PM – 10:00 PM

Our flagship fine dining room seats just 48 guests, ensuring every visit feels personal. Chef-driven tasting menus spotlight the finest seasonal ingredients, complemented by an award-winning wine program and a level of service that anticipates every need.

Sirena Seafood

Fresh from the water,
straight to your table.

Daily  ·  12:00 PM – 10:00 PM

Named for the mythic sea spirit, Sirena celebrates Florida's extraordinary seafood in a relaxed, sun-filled setting. Hook-to-table catches, stone crab in season, and the most celebrated fish tacos on the coast.

The Oyster Deck

Salty air, cold drinks,
perfect oysters.

Daily  ·  11:00 AM – Sunset

The Oyster Deck perches above the waterline with a live raw bar, cold craft beers, and handcrafted cocktails built for the heat. The most social spot at The Bellavera — and the easiest place to lose track of time in the best possible way.
