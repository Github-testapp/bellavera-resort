Accessibility at
The Bellavera

We are committed to an inclusive experience for every guest.

← Back
◈

The Bellavera Resort & Spa is proud to comply with the Americans with Disabilities Act (ADA) and strives to exceed minimum accessibility requirements across all facilities and digital touchpoints.

On-Property Accessibility

  • Accessible rooms and suites with roll-in showers, grab bars, and lowered fixtures — available in Oceanview and Garden categories
  • Wide doorways (minimum 32") and threshold-free entries throughout all accessible rooms
  • Pool lifts at all three resort pools and accessible beach pathway to the Atlantic
  • Fully accessible restaurants, spa, fitness centre, and event spaces
  • Accessible valet and self-parking spaces adjacent to main entrance
  • TDD/TTY availability via front desk on request
  • Visual fire alarms and notification devices in all accessible rooms

Website Accessibility

Our website is designed to conform to Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. We implement semantic HTML, ARIA labels, keyboard navigation support, adequate colour contrast, and descriptive alt text for all meaningful images.

We conduct regular accessibility audits and welcome feedback to help us improve. If you encounter any barrier, please contact us immediately.

Service Animals

Service animals are welcomed throughout the resort in accordance with ADA requirements. Please notify our reservations team prior to arrival so we can ensure appropriate arrangements.

Request Assistance

Our team is trained to assist guests with accessibility needs. To request specific accommodations before your arrival, please contact us at +1 888-235-4100 or accessibility@thebellavera.com.

We value your feedback. To report an accessibility issue or request assistance, please email accessibility@thebellavera.com.
